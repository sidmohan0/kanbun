# Support and implementation help

## Community support

Use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/sidmohan0/kanbun/issues/new/choose) for reproducible bugs,
documentation problems, and feature requests. Include the source commit or release, deployment
mode, reproduction steps, and relevant redacted logs. The issue templates identify the useful
details.

Kanbun is maintained by Sid Mohan and is in active development. Community support and fixes depend
on maintainer availability. There is no guaranteed response time or service-level agreement.
Current support covers development on `main`; older commits have no backport or support-duration
commitment. Review release notes and the [security policy](SECURITY.md) when choosing an update.

Report security vulnerabilities through the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Paid implementation help

I offer paid help setting up Kanbun for engineering teams. Contact
[sid@datafog.ai](mailto:sid@datafog.ai) with your team size, current tracker, Cloudflare experience,
integration requirements, and desired handover date.

A setup engagement can include:

- Deployment into your Cloudflare account with installer-owned resources and configuration.
- Email-link authentication, domain/sender configuration, Turnstile, and owner/member onboarding.
- An installer-owned GitHub App and selected repository-to-team mappings.
- Backup configuration, an isolated restore exercise, and operating documentation.
- An agreed data migration or customization after reviewing its feasibility and scope.

Before work starts, we agree on deliverables, acceptance criteria, pricing, access arrangements,
and responsibilities. Your team supplies a responsible owner, appropriate Cloudflare access,
domain and email administration, repository authorization, and any agreed source data.

A typical setup handover includes the deployment's source commit and configuration inventory,
owner access, a demonstrated issue/member workflow, backup/restore evidence, an operating runbook,
and an operator walkthrough. Your organization owns its infrastructure and accounts.

Ongoing maintenance, upgrades, troubleshooting, and further development are scoped separately.
Any response-time or availability commitment is part of an explicit agreement.
